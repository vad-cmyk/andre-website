Quotes

Quotes are based on what we can see or what you've told us about the job. If the scope changes once we're on site — for example, previously-hidden damage, rot, or damp turns up once old paint or render is stripped back — we'll let you know before doing any extra work and agree a revised price with you first. Quotes are valid for 30 days unless we've said otherwise.

Once you've accepted a quote, we'll agree a start date. We need reasonable access to the property and the work area reasonably clear (furniture moved back from walls, etc.) — let us know in advance if that's going to be a problem and we'll work out a plan together. Payment

For most jobs, payment is due on completion unless we've agreed a different arrangement in writing (for example, a deposit on larger jobs, or payment in stages on longer projects). We accept bank transfer. Invoices are payable within 7 days unless agreed otherwise.

Cancellation & rescheduling

We'd appreciate as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule — ideally at least 48 hours — so we can offer the slot to another job. We're reasonable about genuine problems on your end; repeated late cancellations may incur a reasonable charge to cover lost time.

Materials & colour matching

We'll use the paint brand, colour and finish agreed with you. Where we're colour-matching an existing finish, we do our best to get as close a match as reasonably possible, but small variations can happen — particularly with older, sun-faded or discontinued colours, and especially across lime-based finishes, which weather naturally over time by design. We'll always show you a sample or test patch first on anything where an exact match matters to you.
