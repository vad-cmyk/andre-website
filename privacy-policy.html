Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Plain terms, no legal padding. Last updated 9 August 2026.

Vision Decorators ("we", "us") is a painting and decorating business based at 123 Mile Cross Lane, Norwich, NR6 6RQ. This page explains what information we collect through this website, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we only collect a small amount of information in the first place.

What we collect

When you email us, you may give us some or all of the following:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Whatever you tell us about the job — the service needed, the property, and any details in your message

We use Vercel Analytics to see how many people visit this site and which pages they look at. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't track you individually — it can't identify you, and the same visit looks the same whether it's your first time or your fiftieth. We don't use advertising or cross-site tracking scripts of any kind.

Why we collect it

Purely to respond to your enquiry, quote for the job, and — if you go ahead — arrange and carry out the work. We don't use your details for marketing, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who sees it

Emails come straight to our own inbox — there's no third-party form service or processor involved. Your information isn't shared with anyone else, except where we're legally required to (for example, HMRC records for invoicing).

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to quote and, if the job goes ahead, for our own job and invoicing records — typically no longer than a few years, in line with standard UK bookkeeping requirements. If nothing comes of an enquiry, we don't keep it indefinitely.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's inaccurate
  • Delete your information, where we're not required to keep it for legal reasons
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose

To ask about any of this, email office@vision-decorators.com. If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Changes to this policy

If how we handle information changes, we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
