Door Painting

The first thing anyone sees of your house.

A front door takes more weather and more handling than almost anything else on the outside of your home. We prep it properly and finish it so it stays smooth and glossy — not just for the first six months.

Email us
Navy gloss finish — Norwich

What's included

Prep is most of the job.

01

Rub down & strip

Old flaking coats keyed back or stripped, timber checked for rot and made good before anything else happens.

02

Fill & prime

Joints and dents filled, bare timber or metal primed so the topcoats have something solid to hold onto.

03

Colour match

Any brand or shade — Farrow & Ball, Little Greene, or matched from a sample — sprayed or brushed to your preference.

04

Weatherproof topcoats

Two full topcoats in an exterior-grade finish, so it holds its gloss and colour through a Norfolk winter.
